## 🔥 Flagship Tools

### Smart Fire Alarm Placement Engine
> *Automatically places smoke/heat detectors across an entire floor plan in seconds*

- Reads room geometry, function type, and ceiling height
- Applies **NFPA 72** coverage rules automatically
- Handles above-false-ceiling placement separately
- Generates loop wiring + unique device numbering
- Outputs a **Single Line Diagram** ready for submission

**Impact:** What used to take a draughtsman 2 days now runs in under 2 minutes.

---

### CCTV Field of View Automation
> *Generates accurate FOV hatches for every camera on a floor plan — instantly*

- Reads camera properties: angle, range, orientation
- Creates accurate visual coverage hatches in Revit
- Auto-tags every camera with unique identification numbers
- Maps each camera back to its data outlet source
- Handles parking coverage layouts with sensor + LED bay placement

**Impact:** Eliminates manual FOV drafting entirely. Zero calculation errors.

---

### Typical Floor Replication Tool
> *Design one floor with full circuiting — replicate it across 30 floors in one click*

- Copies circuit design with all electrical parameters intact
- Creates or connects to existing panel schedules automatically
- Maintains view presentation and annotation standards
- Works across linked Revit models

**Impact:** Critical for high-rise projects with repetitive floor plates.

---

### Cable Tray Routing Automation
> *Builds complete cable tray networks along complex geometry*

- Grid-pattern tray generation with fittings and proper spacing
- **Tunnel Follower** — aligns tray along curved tunnel geometry
- Curve & Line Followers for device placement at configurable intervals
- CAT6 length checker flags any data outlet route exceeding 90m

---

### Intelligent Data Outlet Placement
> *Reads all networked equipment in a space and places data outlets automatically*

- Ceiling and wall-mounted variants
- Auto-fills device parameters and tags
- Zones outlets by Floor Distribution / SCN room number
- Outputs complete outlet schedules ready for tender
